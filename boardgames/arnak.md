# Lost Ruins of Arnak

Played on Board Game Arena, 3 players. Compiled 2026-11-16 from the coaching chat of Nov 9.

## Before giving any advice

- Andrew wants a whole-game engine plan, not a best play each turn.
- Icons in screenshots are unreliable (travel icons, resource types, guardian costs). Ask Andrew to hover or tap the element in BGA and confirm the exact cost or effect before he commits.
- Before any purchase or assistant action, check what it spends. A plan died when an assistant's artifact purchase used the only compass needed for a guardian fight.
- Don't tier cards from memory. The community spreadsheet on the BGA forums is the best full reference, though players dispute some of its ratings.

## Rules Claude got wrong

- **Free actions vs main actions**: several free actions (lightning-bolt icon) were called main actions.
- **Travel icons** (ship, car, boot) were confused. A car can pay for a boot.
- **Journal**: its notebook vs magnifying-glass effect was misread.
- **Exploring can wake a guardian**: it does at level II sites. Claude said exploring never does.
- **Torch gives a tablet**, not a compass (corrected more than once).

## Board Game Arena quirks

- "End turn automatically after main action" stops free actions from being played after the main action. Turn it off to queue free actions.
- The Watch's "pass for 3 coins" only works when the Watch itself is played with its pass option as the last action, not with the generic Pass button.

## Lessons learned

- **Points** come from the research track (usually the most), temple tiles, guardians (5 each), idols and cards in the deck, minus Fear.
- **Default engine**: research rush on a lean item deck. Economy in rounds 1 and 2, artifacts and guardians in rounds 3 and 4, research and temple tiles in round 5.
- **The engine came online about a round late.** Causes: too much weight on exile and deck-thinning, not enough on early exploring for idols and on research resources, and losing item-row cards to opponents on timing.
- **Round 1**: items over artifacts; favor items that make resources or give flexible travel. Don't over-buy exile.
- **Temple tiles go fast.** One opponent took several 11-point tiles; reach the temple before the good tiles are gone.
- Items seen and rated in that game: Binoculars, Dog and Machete strongest; Torch and Axe solid; Revolver weaker.
