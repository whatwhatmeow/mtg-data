# Gunsen

Played on Board Game Arena. Compiled 2026-11-16 from the coaching chats of Nov 5 to Nov 16. Andrew has played Scorpion (won, 7 achievements) and Unicorn.

## Before giving any advice

- Andrew wants tap-by-tap instructions: name the exact button, then ask for a screenshot before the tricky part (movement).
- Card symbols are hard to read at screenshot size. Say which ones are unclear and ask.
- Look up the rules instead of guessing.

## Rules Claude got wrong or didn't know

- **The icons along the bottom of an order card are its cost** (wood, coin or banner; a slash means "or"). A card with no icons there is free. Claude first recommended a card without noticing it had a cost.
- **Achievements can be scored from only 2 goal cards in total.** A plan that needs a third goal card is dead once two are in use.
- **Movement can be skipped silently.** After a column pays out, the game waits for an army to be tapped; pressing another button (likely the blue arrow at the top right) ends the step. One turn's movement was lost this way.
- Troop counts and distances were misread from screenshots more than once. Confirm them with Andrew.

## How a turn works (as confirmed in play)

- Each turn: **activate a column OR take a noble action**, not both.
- Order cards stay in their column for the rest of the game and pay out every time that column activates.
- Influence spent on a noble action: 1 = take another order card, 2 = recruit a clan hero and activate any column, 3 = take a Special Order and activate its column.
- In the Influence column, movement follows the payout, and 1 wood buys a second move (seen in the game log).
- Heroes don't count toward the 5-troop limit on a space.
- Yellow arrows are safe moves; orange arrows are attacks.
- "Use influence" is the noble action; "Activate action column" just places the card in its column.

## Lessons learned

- **Influence is the engine.** Stack Influence order cards early, then spend on Special Orders and heroes.
- **Pay coin, not wood**, when a card offers the choice. Wood is scarcer and pays for buildings and second moves.
- **Scorpion line that won**: Influence cards, the Special Order Conscription for troops, the hero Bayushi Kachiko (kills one enemy troop at the start of a battle), then sequence battles and moves to finish achievements (Sting of the Scorpion, Web of Intrigues, shrines, battle wins). Two achievements in one turn is possible.
- **After a column pays out, stop.** Tap the army and look for highlighted hexes before pressing anything else.
- Sacrificing an order card to a noble action also keeps it away from opponents.
- Turn timers are long (35 hours); there is time to send a screenshot before an unfamiliar prompt.
